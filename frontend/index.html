What the verdict means

Every result is bounded to the selected filing and template.

SUPPORTED_BY_FILING
The filing supports the frozen claim within the template’s exact rule.
QUALIFICATION_REQUIRED
The claim needs a material scope or numeric qualification.
OVERSTATED
The claim states more than the selected filing supports.
WRONG_PERIOD_OR_ENTITY
The fetched evidence does not match the frozen filing identity.
NOT_COMPARABLE
The filing does not contain a usable denominator or comparable program description.
UNRESOLVED
Evidence was unavailable or validators could not derive a stable result. It is not an adverse finding.
